ArNet Dashboard Guide

Frequency

The main frequency display uses virtual ArNet frequencies in Vt.

Each integer frequency is one channel. For example: 

5200.00 - 5200.99 = Channel 5200

The ±1.00, ±0.10 and ±0.01 buttons provide coarse, medium and fine tuning.

Channel Sectors

LCS — Lower Channel Sector. Uses approximately .00 through .49. Similar in concept to LSB.

UCS — Upper Channel Sector. Uses approximately .50 through .99. Similar in concept to USB.

FCS — Full Channel Sector. Receives the entire .00 through .99 channel. Similar in concept to full-channel AM.

LCS and UCS can carry separate conversations simultaneously on the same integer channel. An FCS receiver can hear both.

Offset Meter

Shows your decimal position inside the current channel. 

.00       .50       .99
LEFT     CENTER     RIGHT

S-Meter

Displays received signal/audio strength.

Scope

Displays the waveform of received or transmitted audio activity.

Waterfall

Displays activity across the currently selected ArNet band.

Click the waterfall to tune directly to a frequency. Use the zoom controls for precise decimal-frequency tuning.

Off-Frequency Reception

Signals within the same integer channel can still be received while slightly mistuned. The farther the receiver is from the exact transmitting frequency, the more distorted and filtered the audio becomes.

Modes

AARM, ABM and ABMTV control the ArNet transmission mode. A receiver normally must use the same mode as the transmitter.

Bands

The band selector chooses which ArNet band and frequency range the dashboard is using.

Bandwidth

Bandwidth controls the configured virtual receive/transmit width. Stations normally need matching bandwidth settings.

Network

The dashboard connects to the ArNet relay. Audio streams, media transmissions, spectrum activity and station information are shared through the relay.

Morse Decoder

Displays Morse-related decoded information received or generated by compatible ArNet transmissions.
